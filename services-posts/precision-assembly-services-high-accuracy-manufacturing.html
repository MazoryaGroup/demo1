Precision Assembly Services | High-Accuracy Manufacturing

Precision assembly solutions for complex industrial components with strict quality standards, accurate fitting, and dependable production performance.

What We Do

‍

  • Laser Cutting

‍

We utilize super precise CNC laser cutting machines to expertly slice through steel, aluminum, and stainless steel sheets with tolerances as tight as ±0.1mm. Each cut is directly programmed from your CAD file, ensuring there’s no manual guesswork and no dimensional mistakes, providing you with flawless results.

‍

  • Press Brake Bending

‍

Our press brake machines expertly handle bending operations across a full range of angles and profiles. Whether your design requires simple folds or complex multi-bend geometries, our highly skilled operators work to your exact specifications with complete first-article verification.

‍

  • MIG & TIG Welding

‍

We handle both MIG and TIG welding for all sorts of projects, whether you need something super strong or just want it to look nice. Our certified welders meet international standards and double-check each weld joint before proceeding.

‍

  • Punching & Forming

‍

For high-volume sheet metal programs, we use CNC turret punching to produce consistent holes, slots, and forms at speed — without sacrificing dimensional accuracy across large batch runs.

‍

Materials We Work With

‍

We work with mild steel, stainless steel, and aluminum, perfect for all sorts of sheet metal projects in industries like automotive and enclosure manufacturing. We get our materials from trusted suppliers, so you know you're getting quality. Plus, we can provide full traceability documentation if you need it, giving you peace of mind.

‍

No matter if you need the strength of mild steel, the rust resistance of stainless steel, or the lightweight perks of aluminum, we’ve got you covered. Our facility is set up to handle all three materials while keeping our quality top-notch. We also provide detailed documentation for every order, so you can count on us.
