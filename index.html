Got questions?

Know minimum order quantities and shipping timelines before requesting a quote.

What's your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ starts from 50 units, depending on product complexity and material. We'll confirm the exact MOQ with your quote.

How long does it take to get a quote?

We will respond within 24 to 72 hours with unit cost, tooling, lead time, and minimum order quantity. A real engineer carefully reviews every request, ensuring trust in pricing.

Do you sign NDAs before reviewing designs?

Yes. We sign NDAs before receiving any drawings or specs, so your IP stays fully protected.

What certifications do you hold?

We handle ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, IATF 16949, and RoHS compliance. Contact us for certificates!
