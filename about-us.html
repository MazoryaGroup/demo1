Our Story

We’re the engineers to ensure projects meet standards with 20 years of experience.

How we got here

We didn’t start with a fully certified, export-ready facility. We built it gradually—investment by investment, audit by audit—to earn the trust of serious B2B buyers and partners.

We kicked things off in a tiny workshop with just one CNC machine and a straightforward goal — to consistently deliver on our promises. Fast forward twenty years, and that same idea still drives every single order we handle.

Exported to the world

We proudly ship to Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and North America — our extensive and reliable delivery network spans these regions.

Our journey

Building a legacy of precision manufacturing through innovation, trusted partnerships, and continuous growth.

1976

Started with One Machine

We began in 1976 with one CNC lathe and two dedicated people, focused on keeping our promises. Early local customers needed small batches; we delivered early, and they kept returning.

1990

Expanding the Facility

In the 1990s, we reinvested heavily in new machines and certifications. By 2004, our 12,000 sq ft facility successfully handled its first export orders, marking a major milestone achieved in our growth journey.

2005

Certified & Export Ready

ISO 9001 certification in 2005 brought automotive Tier-1 clients, aerospace projects, and global buyers. We built the systems and dedicated team to ensure flawless and timely order fulfillment every single time.

2026

340+ Clients. Still Getting Better

Today we run 50+ machines, hold four active certifications, and ship to 40+ countries. But the standard hasn't changed since 1976 — deliver what we promise, every time. That's still the only metric that matters.

Our mission & values

We believe that manufacturing partnerships should prioritize honesty and openness, rather than merely relying on empty promises. The values we share are not just for show — they fundamentally shape how we approach production every single day.

We're here to be the go-to manufacturing partner that B2B buyers can trust without a second thought. That mission fuels every certification we chase and every process we stick to.

Got questions?

Know minimum order quantities and shipping timelines before requesting a quote.

What's your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ starts from 50 units, depending on product complexity and material. We'll confirm the exact MOQ with your quote.

How long does it take to get a quote?

We will respond within 24 to 72 hours with unit cost, tooling, lead time, and minimum order quantity. A real engineer carefully reviews every request, ensuring trust in pricing.

Do you sign NDAs before reviewing designs?

Yes. We sign NDAs before receiving any drawings or specs, so your IP stays fully protected.

What certifications do you hold?

We handle ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, IATF 16949, and RoHS compliance. Contact us for certificates!
